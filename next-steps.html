What happens next?

Getting started should feel comfortable. Share a little about what you need, choose a time to talk, and bring your questions. There’s no obligation to purchase insurance.

01 · Share

Fill out the contact form.

Tell me what you need, how you’d like to meet, and whether you prefer email or text. Check the permission box and complete the verification check when prompted.

02 · Schedule

Choose your appointment.

After submitting, follow the Calendly link to pick an available time. Finish booking there to confirm your appointment.

03 · Talk

Meet with Shaun.

We’ll begin by listening to what matters to you and talking through your questions. If you’d feel more comfortable with a family member or trusted person joining you, let Shaun know when arranging the appointment.
