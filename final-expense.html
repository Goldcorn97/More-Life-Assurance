What is final expense insurance?

Final expense insurance is life insurance purchased with end-of-life expenses in mind. A policy’s death benefit can help beneficiaries manage expenses after the insured person dies, subject to the policy terms.

Start with your priorities

Consider the expenses you want to help cover, any existing life insurance, and a monthly premium you can comfortably maintain. The optional amounts on our form are starting points for discussion, not quotes.

Feel comfortable with what you choose

We’ll discuss who can qualify, what the policy costs, what it does not cover, and whether any waiting period applies. Take time to review the details and ask questions before deciding.

Before you choose

Questions worth asking.

  • What benefit amount fits my needs and budget?
  • Will the premium change?
  • Is there a waiting period or reduced benefit at first?
  • What happens if I miss a payment?

Bring your questions. We’ll take it one step at a time, with no obligation to purchase.
