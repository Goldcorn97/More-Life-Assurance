Start with understanding.

Understand the main choices

Original Medicare includes Part A and Part B. Medicare Advantage is a private-plan alternative that bundles Part A and Part B, and usually includes prescription drug coverage.

Ask about prescription coverage

Many Medicare Advantage plans include Part D prescription drug coverage. Check the plan’s formulary, pharmacy network, and costs for your prescriptions. This page does not offer standalone Part D or Medicare Supplement policies.

Look at your everyday needs

Discuss your doctors, prescriptions, budget, and current coverage at your appointment. Compare costs and coverage details, and check which enrollment opportunities apply to you before making changes.

Before you choose

Questions worth asking.

  • Are my doctors and hospitals in the plan’s network?
  • Are my prescriptions covered, and at what cost?
  • What would I pay beyond the monthly premium?
  • When am I eligible to enroll or make changes?
