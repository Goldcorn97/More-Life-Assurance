Before we talk

A little preparation.
A more helpful conversation.

Come as you are, with the questions on your mind. This guide can help you feel ready, but you don’t need every document or answer to get started. If you’d like a family member or trusted person to join you, let Shaun know ahead of time.

Keep documents with you for your appointment. Please don’t upload or email medical details, Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or identity documents through this website.

Life insurance and final expense
  • Think about who you want to protect and which expenses you want help covering.
  • Have a comfortable monthly budget in mind, if you know it.
  • If you already have coverage, have your policy summary available for your own reference.
Health insurance
  • Think about who needs coverage and when you would like coverage to begin.
  • Have a list of the doctors, hospitals, and pharmacies that matter to you.
  • Write down questions about premiums, deductibles, and out-of-pocket costs.
Medicare Advantage
  • Have your current coverage information available for reference; don’t submit your Medicare number on this website.
  • Prepare your questions and a private list of your doctors, prescriptions, and preferred pharmacies.
  • Complete the separate Scope of Appointment process with Shaun before a plan marketing appointment, using the requirements for the applicable plan year.

This preparation guide is not a Scope of Appointment or enrollment application. Life insurance, final expense, and annuity discussions require a separate appointment from a Medicare plan marketing appointment.

Retirement and annuity conversations
  • Think about when you hope to retire and your priorities for retirement income.
  • Consider money you may need access to for emergencies or upcoming expenses.
  • Bring questions about insurance options, costs, access to funds, and how a product could fit your goals. Keep account numbers private.
Virtual or in-person meetings
  • For a virtual appointment, choose a quiet place and check the meeting link and your internet connection.
  • For an in-person appointment, confirm the meeting location with Shaun first.
  • If you need an accommodation or help preparing, email info@morelifeassurance.com.
